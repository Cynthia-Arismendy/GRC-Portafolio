# Behavioral Motivation and Reward Architecture

## High Status Incentives for Hybrid and Remote Professionals
For salaried professionals, minor monetary rewards do not drive meaningful behavior change. This framework focuses on professional visibility, team recognition, and operational flexibility tailored for both on site and remote work environments:

### On Site Corporate Employee Incentives
* Preferred Parking Privileges: Monthly top security reporters receive prime parking access.
* Flexible Dining Vouchers: Corporate gift cards allowing team members to select their preferred restaurant for department celebrations.
* Executive Recognition: Quarterly shoutouts from corporate leadership during all hands meetings.

### Remote Employee Incentives
* Meal Delivery Vouchers: DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub credits allowing remote employees to order lunch from their favorite local restaurant during virtual team celebrations.
* Work From Anywhere Extension Pass: Subject to corporate compliance policies, top security performers earn up to three additional Work From Anywhere days, allowing greater location flexibility.
* Security Cleared Travel Authorization: Prioritized Information Technology and Governance review for temporary international remote work requests upon completing advanced data protection modules.
* Total Disconnect Day: One fully paid unplugged day off for employees maintaining zero simulation click rates across two consecutive quarters.
* Digital Flexibility Passes: Priority access to early Friday sign offs and no meeting focus blocks.
* Home Office Technology Stipends: Digital vouchers for ergonomic accessories, secondary monitors, or noise canceling headsets.
* Virtual Recognition Badges: Custom Slack and Microsoft Teams profile indicators highlighting top security defenders.

## Participatory Design Survey
Prior to campaign rollout, Human Resources distributes a brief survey gathering feedback on desired incentives within approved company budgets. Involving employees in program design builds immediate ownership and excitement across distributed teams.

## Viral Culture and Social Amplification Strategy
To transform security awareness into an engaging internal trend, the framework incorporates viral marketing mechanics within corporate communication channels such as Slack and Microsoft Teams:

* Corporate Hashtag Campaign: Employees share completion milestones using the campaign hashtag CatchTheSnowflake, triggering automated public recognition feeds.
* Peer Challenge System: Upon completing monthly episodes, users can send friendly challenges to team members, earning bonus speed points for rapid team completion.
* Security Ambassador Recognition: Monthly awards highlight top internal promoters who drive engagement and conversation around current threat trends.
* Monthly Episode Drops: Highlighting new micro learning content with visual teasers creates anticipation similar to episode premieres, sustaining company wide engagement.
