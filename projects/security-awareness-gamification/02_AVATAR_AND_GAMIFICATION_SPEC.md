# Dynamic Avatar System and Professional Credentialing

## Visual Avatar Progression
Employees interact with a personalized digital avatar that reflects their security posture:
* Level One Baseline: Standard professional avatar attire.
* Level Five Sentinel: Unlocks advanced digital defense accessories after completing core modules.
* Level Ten Security Master: Unlocks the full security suite graphic, representing complete framework compliance.

## Professional LinkedIn Integration
Upon completing ten dynamic modules, employees earn a verified Master Security Ambassador badge. This digital credential details specific competency in identifying modern artificial intelligence threats. Employees can export this verified credential to their public professional profiles, enhancing their resume while demonstrating company wide compliance.
