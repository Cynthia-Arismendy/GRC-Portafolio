# Pedagogical Framework: The Phishing Snowflake Effect

## Instructional Design Philosophy
Adult learning requires relevance, immediate application, and emotional engagement. Rather than punishing mistakes, this framework uses micro learning and immediate visual feedback to explain the domino effect of credential exposure.

## Storyboard Script: The Phishing Snowflake Effect

Time: 0:00 to 0:10
Visual: Employee receives an urgent message with a fake QR code for mandatory benefits updates.
Voiceover: It takes only three seconds. An urgent notification, a recognized logo, and one quick scan.
Technical Reality: Social engineering exploiting urgency and brand impersonation.

Time: 0:11 to 0:25
Visual: Employee enters credentials on a convincing fake login portal.
Voiceover: You entered your password and authentication code, but the attacker captured your active session token.
Technical Reality: Adversary in the Middle attack bypassing multi factor authentication.

Time: 0:26 to 0:40
Visual: A small snowball begins rolling down a hill, knocking over blocks labeled Access Control and Data Storage.
Voiceover: From one stolen session key, the attacker moves silently across cloud systems, reaching sensitive records.
Technical Reality: Privilege escalation and unauthorized lateral movement.

Time: 0:41 to 0:55
Visual: The snowball grows into a massive avalanche impacting the corporate office building.
Voiceover: What started as a quick scan becomes operational downtime, regulatory fines, and lost customer trust.
Technical Reality: Enterprise ransomware impact and compliance non conformity.

Time: 0:56 to 1:00
Visual: Screen resets to a green defense interface with three clear action steps.
Voiceover: Stop the snowflake before the avalanche. Pause, verify, and report suspicious requests instantly.
Technical Reality: Active defense culture and proactive incident reporting.
